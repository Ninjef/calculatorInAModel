🎰 I caught myself treating my AI research agent like a slot machine.

I've been running experiments on embedding a calculator inside a transformer — letting AI agents continue the research with only light check-ins from me. The setup is incredible. I can pursue ideas in a field I haven't fully mastered, at a pace I never could alone.

But here's the trap: when the agent can just keep going, every inconclusive result becomes a reason to pull the handle one more time. Maybe the next run works. Maybe the next config. Maybe…

That's not research. That's gambling. 🎲

Real research demands you stop, sit with the new facts, and think carefully before the next move. 🧠 The hands-off workflow makes that pause feel optional — and our brains are wired to skip anything optional that requires effort.

The lesson I'm taking away: the bottleneck in AI-assisted research isn't compute or agent capability. It's the human discipline to slow down when the machine is begging you to speed up. ⏸️

If your agents feel like they're "almost there" — that's exactly when you should close the laptop and think. 💡
