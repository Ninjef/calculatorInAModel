The entire purpose of this experiment and repo is to discover whether a model can learn to use a calculator that's embedded directly into the neural architecture.

Everything else should be inservice to this overarching goal.

# WHY
Models aren't very good at deterministic logic. Not only boolean logic, but mathematical logic as well. The theory here is that by embedding a calculator directly into the model's ability to think, we can give the model a superpower that humans don't even have; the ability to leverage mathematical reasoning directly in its thoughts.

# PROBLEMS
Neural networks learn in a differential way. A calculator is non-differential. We must overcome this difference.
